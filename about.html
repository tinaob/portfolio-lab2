About Me

My first real encounter with computers was in elementary school, where we were taught MS-DOS and how to use floppy disks. I remember being intrigued by how the whole system worked, and that curiosity is what first made me want to become a computer engineer.

As a teenager, I worked as an assistant office administrator, helping with the day-to-day running of the office, including booking travel and reservations for the CEO and managers. Learning more about computers as a young adult only deepened my interest, especially in building, debugging, investigating and solving technical problems, which is part of why I ended up pursuing a Master's degree in Computer Engineering, specializing in Cybersecurity.

My goal is to become an expert across most domains of cybersecurity, as well as software development and testing, and to eventually be in a position where I can mentor others in the field.

My Journey

2007 to 2016: Project Manager / Senior Scrum Master, Zenith Bank

For nearly a decade, I managed technology project delivery in a regulated banking environment, coordinating engineering teams through software development, testing, and deployment across multiple concurrent programmes. Over this period, I wore many hats, moving fluidly between Scrum Master and Project Manager responsibilities depending on what each team and project needed.

Oct 2019 to Jun 2021: Agile Project Manager, Scrum Master and Software Tester, Zanerio Group AB

I led Agile ceremonies and coached teams on Scrum principles to build self-management and delivery discipline. I also ran functional, regression and UAT test cases, manually and with Selenium-based automation, and tracked defects in Jira with developers until the fixes were verified. As Scrum Master and proxy project manager, I supported backlog management and sprint planning so the team always had clear, prioritised work.

May 2022 to Nov 2023: Scrum Master (Consultant), Randstad at Volvo Cars

I worked embedded within software engineering and DevOps teams, gaining a deep, practical understanding of how cross functional teams design, implement, test, and deploy modern software products using Agile and continuous delivery practices. I used Azure DevOps and Jira daily for sprint tracking, backlog management, and delivery reporting.

A challenge I worked on: I worked with a cross-functional team where people had different priorities, and dependencies, risks and changing requirements made it hard to keep delivery on track. I ran the Scrum ceremonies, helped the team spot blockers, and worked with stakeholders to clarify priorities. I made risks and dependencies visible so the team could focus on the most important work. The team got a clearer view of its work and blockers, and people felt able to raise concerns and solve them together.

Dec 2023 to Jun 2024: Operational Excellence Manager (Consultant), RECAS AB at Volvo Group

I coordinated improvement initiatives across business and technical stakeholders at Volvo Group, tracking deliverables, risks and dependencies. I supported operational risk reporting with Power BI dashboards and Microsoft 365 tools, and helped improve workflows to reduce friction between teams.

A challenge I worked on: Operational information and processes had gaps and inconsistencies. When information is incomplete or spread across different sources, it is hard for stakeholders to see the situation and make informed decisions. I analysed the data, found the inconsistencies and process gaps, and helped set up clearer reporting and performance metrics. I worked with stakeholders to understand the problems and share my findings, and we looked for ways to improve the process and automate parts of it. The reporting gave better visibility of operational performance and data quality, and the gaps and improvement opportunities became easier to see.

Aug 2024 to Dec 2024: Project Coordinator (Contract), Busch Vakuumteknik AB

I built detailed project plans with clear goals, scope, timelines and milestones, and kept internal teams, business leads and external partners informed of progress, changes and decisions. I created dashboards and structured reports in Microsoft 365 so leadership could see project status, kept governance and change management records, and tracked risks, issues and actions, escalating when needed to keep delivery on track.

Jan 2025 to Aug 2025: Cybersecurity Analyst (Contract), Zanerio Group AB

This marked my shift from project delivery into hands on technical security work. I designed and built Python automation tools end to end, including an AI powered threat intelligence platform and a cloud security misconfiguration scanner, integrating REST APIs and using the Claude AI API directly in real development workflows, not just in theory.

A challenge I worked on: I was working with security information from many places at once: user accounts, access rights, applications, vulnerabilities and security logs. It was hard to see the gaps and decide what needed attention first. I compared the sources, checked access rights and security controls, and found misclassified accounts and access that was unnecessary or orphaned. I used Splunk for monitoring and Python to automate part of the analysis, and I documented the findings and tracked the fixes. In the end the gaps were easier to see, the quality of the information improved, and stakeholders had clearer information about the risks and what needed to be done.

Sep 2025 to Present: Full Stack Engineer/Cybersecurity Analyst, Winnio AB

I build and maintain full stack features for a browser extension product and a learning workspace platform, working across frontend and backend in an Agile sprint cycle. On the frontend I use semantic HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript and React, and I integrate REST APIs. On the backend I write Python and Node.js for data processing, API integration and automation, and I work with SQL and Google Cloud. I also do cybersecurity work, including risk assessments, reviewing code and configurations for security issues, and security configuration reviews across Google Workspace, GitHub and Slack, with controls aligned to ISO 27001 and GDPR.
